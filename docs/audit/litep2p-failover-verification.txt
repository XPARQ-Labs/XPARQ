Behavior:
- Accepted peers enter a bounded FIFO queue (at most 32).
- Serial tip polling on the three-second tick rotates peers, skipping cooldowns.
- A session without verified progress expires after 60 seconds.
- Existing 15-second request and 24-hour absolute session deadlines remain.
- Header validation, fresh prefix verification and durable body commits count
  as progress. Invalid messages and queued requests do not.
- Session failure releases requests/slot and preserves durable staging.

Live Unix failover scenario:
Mine a three-block source chain and copy it before transport identity generation.
Target downloads its first body from source A. SIGSTOP freezes the child source
process without deliberately closing its connection. A separate source B connects
with a different identity. Target times out A, selects B, revalidates headers and
resumes at least one (but fewer than all three) previously committed bodies.
Canonical tip and miner balance match B; target retains the tip after restart.
Test child cleanup kills and waits for the paused process.
