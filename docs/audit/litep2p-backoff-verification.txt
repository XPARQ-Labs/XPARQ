litep2p sync retry hardening — 2026-10-04

Changes:
- Preserve per-peer retry history through connection/substream closure.
- Guard immediate tip requests, periodic requests and configured redials.
- Repeated tracked failures double cooldown from 30 seconds to 30 minutes.
- Clear history after a successfully completed verified sync session.
- Keep at most 64 histories; expire one hour after the last cooldown ends.

Limitations:
- Local process history; node restart resets it.
- Bounded eviction and changing peer identities can bypass old history.
- Backoff covers local/network failures as well as invalid responses.
- Announcement relay and inbound serving are not rate-limited by this change.
